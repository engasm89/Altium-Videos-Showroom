## Devil’s advocate (read before sending)

Sending **before** PostHog/GA are live means you can show a serious **asset**, not **results**. That is still a strong reopen — but only if the email does not fake demand.

Two honest frames (both used softly above):

1. **Review the asset now** — invite feedback on the hub + reel; discuss campaign use once metrics accumulate.  
2. **Asset ready for campaigns** — structure, Develop story, and tracked CTAs are in place; analytics forthcoming so you can report interest properly.

Do **not** imply Impact/My Activity proves global traffic. Do **not** lead with “please hire me again.” Lead with: independent investment since the prior program ended → here is the asset → let’s discuss feature-awareness / structured education.

If Altium asks for numbers on the first reply, the correct answer is: instrumentation and UTMs are ready; we will share dashboards after keys are live and traffic has run — not invented counts from localStorage.
